About me

The video displayed is a promo for a band I toured with from 2000 to 2002. The video quality is a reflection of that time period.

I began playing drums at age 11, by age 17 I was working in blues and wedding bands in NY. In 2000 I attended State University of New York and obtained an Associates Music Degree. Since then I have toured with rock, reggae and jazz projects. I have also played in Fusion Jazz, Punk & Hardcore bands. While living in San Diego, Ca, I taught drumset at "A Reason To Survive" a non-profit for terminally ill children.

Unfortunitaly, exhaustion and a career fork in the road caused a 10yr hiatus from playing. I have now spent the last year regaining my chops, meter & skill set. I am in the process of recording a video drumset method series from my studio that will be available later this year.
